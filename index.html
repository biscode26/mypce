International Baccalaureate Programme

MYP Learning Engagement

MYP learning engagement encourages students to connect classroom learning with real-world experiences. Through community involvement, projects, personal inquiry and academic achievements, students develop knowledge, skills and a deeper understanding of their role in the wider community.

Community Engagement

Community engagement provides students with opportunities to participate in meaningful activities that create a positive impact on their school and wider community.

Student Participation

Students take part in initiatives, awareness campaigns, service activities and collaborative experiences that connect learning with real-world needs.

Community Project — MYP 3

The Community Project gives MYP 3 students an opportunity to explore a community need and develop a meaningful response through research, planning and action.

Project Focus

Students identify a community issue, investigate its causes and develop a project that demonstrates collaboration, communication and responsible action.

Personal Project — MYP 5

The Personal Project is an opportunity for MYP 5 students to explore an area of personal interest and demonstrate the knowledge and skills they have developed throughout the MYP.

Student Inquiry

Students independently investigate a topic, establish a goal, develop a product or outcome and reflect on their learning journey.

Academic Highlights

Academic highlights celebrate student achievements, accomplishments and learning experiences across different subject areas and grade levels.

Celebrating Achievement

This section can showcase competitions, exhibitions, awards, subject achievements, student presentations and other academic accomplishments.
